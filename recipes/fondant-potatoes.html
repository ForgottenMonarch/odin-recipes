Fondant Potatoes Recipe

Description

Fondant potatoes are an amazing French side that can go with many entrees. They are potatoes that have been cut into small cylinders and pan fried on both sides to give them a golden brown color. Then they are roasted in the oven in a stock with a butter sauce.

Ingredients

  • 3 large russet potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons of high heat resistant vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons of butter
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 1/2 cup of beef broth

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. Cut off both ends of your russet potatoes. Stand the potatoes on one end, and peel them from top to bottom to make them a cylinder. Then lay the potatoe on its side and cut it in half to make 2 cylinders that are about 2 inches long. Repeat for all potatoes.
  3. Soak the potatoes in cold water for about 5 minutes to remove starch from the outside.
  4. Heat your vegetable oil in a heavy oven proof pan over high heat until it shimmers slightly
  5. Pat dry the potatoes with a paper towel, and place the best looking end into the oil. Reduce the heat to medium-high and pan-fry the potatoes until well browned, 5-6 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Flip the potatoes and repeat on the other ends. As they cook, use a paper towel held with tongs to carefully blot out oil from the skillet. Add butter and thyme spriges to the pan.
  7. Pick up the thyme sprig with tongs and use it to pain butter over the top of the potatoes, and cook them until the butter foam turns to a plae taan color. Season with more salt and pepper, and pour the beef stock into the pan.
  8. Transfer the pan to the preheated oven and cook until you can poke through the potatoes with a fork with no resistance, about 30 minutes. If the potatoes aren't tender add 1/4 cup more stock and cook for 10 more minutes.
  9. Place the potatoes on a plate, and spoon the remaining thyme-scented butter over them. Let them color for 5 minutes before serving.
